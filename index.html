Compliance Check

Before you spend money on interiors, the AI reviews your floor plan and the proposed layout, and flags anything that may not meet common building requirements.

  • Room sizes & heights: compared with typical minimums for habitable rooms, kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Light & ventilation: window-to-floor area and cross-ventilation in each room.
  • Clearances & circulation: door widths, passages, and space around furniture.
  • Safety & egress: clear exit paths, and kitchen and gas placement.
Example flags
⚠Bedroom 2: wardrobe narrows the passage near the door
⚠Study: window area looks low for natural light

Checks are based on common guidelines such as India's National Building Code and typical local bylaws. Flags are for review by a qualified professional; this is not a certification or approval.
